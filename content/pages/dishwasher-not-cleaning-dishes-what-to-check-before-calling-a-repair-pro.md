Dishes coming out with food stuck on, a gritty feel, or a cloudy film usually don't mean the dishwasher is dying. The manufacturers point to the same short list first: a dirty filter, blocked spray arms, water that isn't hot enough, the wrong detergent or cycle, and how the racks are loaded. All of those are free to check. Work through them in order before you book a repair.

> **Safety first.** Wait until the dishwasher has cooled down before you reach inside. Frigidaire's use and care manual warns that the heating element can burn you if you touch it during or right after a cycle ([Frigidaire](https://frigidaire.bynder.com/asset/2c5aaeb3-e54a-4baa-9d56-318a5f19bcb1/A29714601en-pdf.pdf)). Look before you grab: KitchenAid says toothpicks, olive pits, and **broken glass** can collect around the filter, so pick those out carefully by hand first ([KitchenAid](https://producthelp.kitchenaid.com/Dishwashers/Dishwasher/Other/Cleaning_and_Odor/Cleaning_and_Maintenance/Cleaning_the_Filter/Cleaning_the_Removable_Filtration_System_-_Dishwasher)). Never run the dishwasher with the filters out. If you need to work on wiring, the water line, or anything behind the kickplate, turn off the breaker and close the water valve first, or call a technician.

## Step 1: Clean the filter

A clogged filter is the first thing Whirlpool, GE, Samsung, and LG all mention.

- **Signs it needs cleaning.** Whirlpool lists dirty dishes, bad odors, visible soil on the filter, worse cleaning, and dishes that feel gritty ([Whirlpool](https://producthelp.whirlpool.com/Dishwashers/Product_Info/Dishwasher_Cleaning_and_Care/How_to_Clean_the_Dishwasher_Filters)).
- **How often.** Whirlpool says once a month, or every two weeks if you run it daily or have a big household. GE says to clean removable fine and ultra fine filters once a month ([GE Appliances](https://products.geappliances.com/appliance/gea-support-search-content?contentId=17452)). Some models have a self-cleaning filter with nothing to remove; your owner's manual tells you which type you have.
- **How.** Pull out the lower rack. On many models the filter twists a quarter turn counterclockwise and lifts out (Whirlpool, LG). Rinse it under running water and use a soft brush for stubborn bits. Whirlpool says not to use a wire brush or scouring pad, because they can damage the filter.
- **Lock it back in.** Whirlpool says if the upper filter assembly turns freely, it isn't locked. LG says a filter that isn't reinstalled properly can't collect debris, which hurts wash performance ([LG](https://www.lg.com/us/support/help-library/lg-dishwasher-maintenance--1433962981524)).

## Step 2: Check the spray arms

- **Spin them by hand.** After loading, spin the middle spray arm by hand before you start. Whirlpool says if you feel resistance, rearrange the dishes ([Whirlpool](https://producthelp.whirlpool.com/Dishwashers/Dishwasher/Operation/Spray_Arms_Not_Working/Spray_Arm_Not_Working_-_Dishwasher)). Samsung gives the same advice for pot handles or anything else sticking out ([Samsung](https://www.samsung.com/us/support/answer/ANS10004786/)).
- **Clear clogged holes.** GE says to loosen debris in the wash arm jets with a paper clip or tweezers, being careful not to damage the jets. Samsung says clogged nozzle holes reduce cleaning and leave food on dishes. Its steps: remove the upper nozzle (many twist off counterclockwise), scrub it with a small brush, soak it overnight in distilled white vinegar, rinse it, and check that it spins after you reinstall it ([Samsung](https://www.samsung.com/us/support/answer/ANS10002330/)).
- **Hard water buildup.** Whirlpool says hard water minerals build up on the spray arms and dishes, and it strongly recommends a water softener if your hardness is 15 grains or more. It suggests a monthly dishwasher cleaner.
- **Only one rack getting clean?** Whirlpool says the Top Rack Only setting turns off the lower spray arm. GE says to make sure Single Rack or Wash Zones isn't selected.

## Step 3: Check the water temperature and supply

- **Hot water matters.** GE says hot water dissolves and activates the detergent, and the water heater should deliver at least 120°F. GE's FAQ gives a range of 120°F to 150°F and says water below 120°F can keep tablets from fully dissolving, which leaves a greasy film ([GE Appliances](https://www.geappliances.com/ge/service-and-support/faq-dishwasher.htm)). LG also recommends 120°F.
- **Test it.** GE says to run the hot water at the kitchen sink and measure it with a candy or meat thermometer.
- **Run the sink first.** Whirlpool says to run hot water at the nearest sink for at least 30 seconds before starting a cycle ([Whirlpool](https://www.whirlpool.com/blog/kitchen/dishwasher-not-cleaning.html)). GE adds: don't run the dishwasher right after a shower or filling a bathtub.
- **Valve and pressure.** Whirlpool says the water supply valve must be completely open, and both Whirlpool and GE say water pressure should be 20 to 120 psi. Whirlpool also says a flood-prevention device that isn't installed correctly can stop water from reaching the dishwasher ([Whirlpool](https://producthelp.whirlpool.com/Dishwashers/Dishwasher/Wash_Performance/None_of_the_Dishes_are_Clean/Dishes_are_not_Clean)).
- **New install?** GE says to make sure the dishwasher is connected to the hot line, not the cold one, that it's level, and that the drain hose isn't installed higher than 72 inches.

## Step 4: Check the detergent, rinse aid, and cycle

- **Dishwasher detergent only.** Whirlpool, GE, and LG all say to use only automatic dishwasher detergent. Whirlpool says other soaps can cause suds to overflow, and LG says hand dish soap fills the dishwasher with suds and can make it leak ([LG](https://www.lg.com/us/support/help-library/lg-dishwasher-using-detergent-and-additives--20152041517610)).
- **Fresh detergent, right amount.** Whirlpool says fresh detergent cleans better, and to store it tightly closed. It also says too much detergent can leave residue. GE says soft water needs less detergent and hard water needs more.
- **Use rinse aid.** Whirlpool and GE both say rinse aid helps with drying and with hard water film.
- **Pick a heavier cycle.** Whirlpool and GE say short or light cycles may not clean heavily soiled loads. Use a heavier cycle or a heat option like High Temp, Hot Start, or Wash Heat Boost if you have one.

## Step 5: Load it so the water can reach everything

- **Don't overload.** Whirlpool says overloading restricts water circulation and leaves dishes dirty.
- **Face the mess toward the spray.** Whirlpool says to load dishes soiled side down and inward toward the spray arms. Samsung says to put cups and pots face down.
- **Scrape, and know your model.** Whirlpool and GE say to scrape off food but skip heavy pre-rinsing, because some newer dishwashers have sensors that shorten the cycle if dishes look too clean. LG's guide says to soak burnt pots or baked-on egg for about an hour and pre-rinse them first. Your owner's manual is the tiebreaker for your model.
- **Some things never come out right.** LG lists small plastics, crystal, aluminum, silver, and wooden items as poor fits for the dishwasher ([LG](https://www.lg.com/us/support/help-library/lg-dishwasher-dishes-are-not-clean-after-washing--20155101652263)).

## Step 6: Clean the inside

- **Samsung:** put an 8 ounce cup of distilled white vinegar upright in the lower rack and run a Sanitize cycle, or use the Self Clean cycle if your model has one.
- **LG:** run the Machine Clean cycle with citric acid or a dishwasher cleaner in the detergent dispenser.
- **Whirlpool:** recommends a dishwasher cleaner monthly.

Check your owner's manual for your model's cleaning cycle before you start.

## When to stop and call a pro

- **Dishes are still dirty** after you've cleaned the filter and spray arms, confirmed 120°F water, and tried a heavier cycle with fresh detergent. GE says service is needed if its suggestions don't fix the problem.
- **A spray arm won't spin** even with nothing in its way, or it's cracked.
- **Water never seems to get hot,** or a Sanitize light keeps blinking. Samsung says a blinking Sanitize light means the water temperature requirement wasn't met. Raise the supply temperature, and call for service if your water is hot enough and it still blinks.
- **Water stays in the bottom** after the cycle. That's a drain problem; see our [dishwasher not draining guide](/dishwasher-not-draining-what-to-check-before-calling-a-repair-pro/).
- **The filter or a part is cracked** and you aren't comfortable replacing it, or the fix means getting behind the kickplate or near wiring.
- **It's still under warranty or a protection plan.** Check that before booking anyone else.

## What does it cost?

These are published averages, not quotes. Your price depends on your city, the part, and the diagnostic fee.

- **Whirlpool** lists average out-of-warranty dishwasher repairs at **$200 to $450** for parts and labor. It says these are 2020 averages from its W Service network in six metro areas (Baltimore, Boston, Orlando, Birmingham, Seattle, and New York), and that actual costs vary ([Whirlpool](https://www.whirlpool.com/repair/service.html)).
- **Sears Home Services** says the average dishwasher repair can be **$150 to $200**, and that many dishwasher repairs cost less than $250. It's a repair company, so treat these as vendor estimates ([Sears Home Services](https://www.searshomeservices.com/repair/dishwasher-repair-service)).

A dirty filter or clogged spray arm costs nothing to fix yourself, so do the checks above first. If you book someone, ask for a written estimate and whether the diagnostic fee counts toward the repair.

## How to find a repair service

Start with the brand. Its service line knows your model and warranty. These links were all checked on November 27, 2026:

- **Whirlpool:** [online scheduling](https://www.whirlpool.com/repair/service.html).
- **GE Appliances:** [schedule through Bodewell](https://products.geappliances.com/appliance/gea-support-search-content?contentId=16875).
- **Samsung:** [repair request portal](https://www.samsung.com/us/support/service/), or call 1-800-SAMSUNG.
- **LG:** [repair request page](https://www.lg.com/us/support/repair-service/schedule-repair).
- **Any brand:** [Sears Home Services](https://www.searshomeservices.com/repair/dishwasher-repair-service) offers online booking for dishwasher repair.

Have your model and serial number ready. Tell the scheduler what the dishes look like (food left on, gritty, or cloudy film), whether it's both racks or just one, your sink's hot water temperature, any error code or blinking light, and what you already cleaned. Hours and phone numbers change, so confirm on the linked page before you call.
