A washer that leaves your clothes soaking wet, or fills with water and then just sits there, is one of the most common laundry problems. Most of the time it isn't broken. The manufacturers say the usual causes are an unbalanced load, a lid or door that isn't fully closed, a washer that isn't level, too many suds, or a drain problem, because a washer won't spin until it has drained. This guide walks you through those checks in order, and tells you when to stop and call a technician.

If water is sitting in the tub, start with our guide to a [washer that won't drain](/washer-wont-drain-what-to-check-before-calling-a-repair-pro/). This page focuses on the spin and agitation side.

> **Safety first.** GE says to unplug the washer before any maintenance or cleaning, to lower the risk of electric shock ([GE Appliances](https://products.geappliances.com/appliance/gea-support-search-content?contentId=17398)). If there's water on the floor near the outlet, turn off the washer's circuit breaker instead of reaching for the plug. Keep your hands out of the drum until it has fully stopped. Never force a locked door open. If you smell burning or the breaker trips, stop and call a qualified technician.

## Step 1: Is it really stuck?

- **Wait a few minutes.** GE says its full-size top-loaders can pause for up to 3 minutes between parts of a cycle, and that pumping out can take another 3 minutes before the spin starts. Don't turn the dial forward by hand during a pause, or you may skip the pump-out ([GE Appliances](https://products.geappliances.com/appliance/gea-support-search-content?contentId=23082)).
- **Is it agitating quietly?** GE says newer top-loaders are much quieter than older ones. Its test: put one item in, note where it sits, close the lid, let it run for a minute or two, and look. If the item moved, the washer is agitating. GE also says newer models can take 20 to 30 seconds after filling to sense the load before the drive starts.
- **Check the spin setting.** Whirlpool says Low Spin or No Spin leaves more water in the load, and that the washer remembers the last settings used for each cycle, so a "no spin" choice can carry over to your next load. Delicate and hand wash cycles also spin slowly on purpose ([Whirlpool](https://producthelp.whirlpool.com/Laundry/Washers/Front_Load_Washers/Not_Starting_or_Not_Operating/Not_Spinning/Not_Spinning_-_Front_Load_Washer)).
- **Check for a soak cycle or a knob between settings.** GE says a soak option can add 15 or 30 minutes before the wash. On models with an Options or speed knob, it must point at a setting, not sit between two.
- **Did the power blink?** Whirlpool says a power failure during spin can stop the washer from reaching full speed. GE says to check the breaker or fuse and test the outlet with a small lamp.

## Step 2: Fix an unbalanced load (the most common cause)

Washers slow down or stop spinning when the weight in the drum is lopsided, to protect themselves. Whirlpool says a top-loader will try to fix it on its own by refilling and gently rocking the load, and shows an error if that fails ([Whirlpool](https://producthelp.whirlpool.com/Laundry/Washers/Top_Load_Washer/Cycle_Concerns/Not_Spinning_-_Washer)).

- **Know the codes.** LG shows a lowercase **uE** while it tries to rebalance. After 3 tries it changes to **UE** and needs you to fix the load ([LG](https://www.lg.com/us/support/help-library/lg-washer-what-is-a-top-load-washing-machine-ueub-error-code--1394563702295)). On GE top-loaders from 2015 and newer, a flashing **SPIN** light or "**bALAnCINg**" in the display means the load needs redistributing.
- **Spread the load out.** Pause the washer, open it, untangle the items, and spread them evenly around the drum. Then press Start, or choose Drain & Spin. GE says loading a top-loader beyond the balance ring can make it shake and stop spinning.
- **Avoid single heavy items.** Whirlpool and LG both say one rug, one blanket, or one bulky item is hard to balance. Add a few towels, or wash two or three similar items together.
- **Small loads count too.** LG and Samsung say a very small load can bunch up on one side. Add items or lower the spin speed.
- **Use the right cycle.** Whirlpool says bedding and bulky items need the Bulky or Sheets/Bedding cycle, and that a low-water cycle can stop clothes from moving well ([Whirlpool](https://producthelp.whirlpool.com/Laundry/Washers/Top_Load_Washer/Cycle_Concerns/Not_Agitating_-_Washer)).

## Step 3: Make sure the washer is level

Whirlpool and LG both say a washer that isn't level can unbalance the load and stop a proper spin. LG's test is to put your hands on diagonal corners and try to rock the washer. If it rocks, adjust the legs on that side with a wrench until all four feet are firm on the floor ([LG](https://www.lg.com/us/support/help-library/lg-washer-troubleshooting-washing-machine-drum-not-spinning--20150584818000)). On GE space-saving top-loaders, GE says to twist the leveling feet down to the floor, then lock them with the locking nuts. Samsung says to level a pedestal first, keep about an inch of space around the washer, and make sure the floor is stable ([Samsung](https://www.samsung.com/us/support/troubleshoot/TSG10003485/)).

## Step 4: Check the lid or door

- **Top-loaders.** GE says none of its top-loaders will spin with the lid up, and that the washer won't agitate or spin if the lid switch probe or magnet on the lid is missing or damaged. The probe is a small plastic piece on the underside of the lid, and the magnet is a plastic-coated piece held by two screws. GE Factory Service adds that a lid switch can be knocked loose by spin vibration ([GE Appliances Factory Service](https://repair.geappliances.com/resources/faq/article/washer-troubleshooting-guide)).
- **Front-loaders.** Whirlpool says the door switch lets the washer run only when the door is shut, so make sure nothing is caught in the door.
- **GE space-saving top-loaders.** GE says **dE** or **E2** means the lid was opened. Close the lid and press Start/Pause.

## Step 5: Too many suds

Whirlpool says non-HE detergent, or too much HE detergent, can make enough suds to slow or stop the spin. Use only HE detergent in an HE washer, and measure it.

## Step 6: Make sure it can drain

A washer won't spin with water still in the tub. Samsung says the washer tries to drain before it spins, and if it can't drain, it never gets to the spin step ([Samsung](https://www.samsung.com/us/support/troubleshoot/TSG10003486/)).

- **Front-loaders:** clean the pump filter. Samsung says to do this about once a month or every 40 washes, whichever comes first. Put down towels and a shallow pan, drain the water through the small drain tube, then twist the filter counterclockwise to remove it.
- **Drain hose:** check for kinks and clogs. Samsung says the hose should go 6 to 8 inches into the standpipe, without an airtight seal. Whirlpool's top-load guide says about 4.5 inches. Follow your own installation manual.
- **Cold garages:** GE says water in the pump can freeze and lock the motor if the washer sits below freezing. Let it thaw before you try again.
- **Foreign objects:** LG says coins, zippers, or buttons can get trapped and affect the drum. Remove any you can see with pliers or tweezers.

Our [washer won't drain guide](/washer-wont-drain-what-to-check-before-calling-a-repair-pro/) covers the drain checks in more detail.

## Step 7: Try a reset or test cycle

- **GE Hydrowave top-loaders (generally select 2011 to 2015 models):** GE says to unplug the washer for 1 minute, plug it back in, then lift and lower the lid 6 times within 12 seconds. Start within 30 seconds, raise the lid at least 2 inches each time, and close it fully. Then run a small cycle with no clothes to test it ([GE Appliances](https://products.geappliances.com/appliance/gea-support-search-content?contentId=17391)). GE says some error messages clear by choosing Drain & Spin, or by unplugging the washer for 1 minute.
- **Samsung front-loaders:** Samsung says Calibration mode can get the spin working again, especially after a drain issue is fixed. Empty the drum, then on most models press and hold **Temp.** and **Delay End** for 3 seconds until **Cb** appears. If your manual doesn't list Calibration mode, your model doesn't have it.
- **LG:** run a spin-only test with the drum empty. LG says if the drum spins, adjust the load size and try again. If it still won't spin, LG says it needs service.

## Parts and tools that commonly help with this

These are links to Amazon search results, not specific products, so you pick the exact part. **Match any part to your washer's model number** (usually on a label near the door or lid, or on the back) and check it against your owner's manual. **Unplug the washer and shut off the water before any repair.** Buy a part only after the free checks above point to it.

- [Appliance leveling tool or bubble level](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=appliance+bubble+level&tag=applianceh06-20): for checking that the washer is level in Step 3.
- [Adjustable wrench](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=adjustable+wrench&tag=applianceh06-20): LG and GE say to adjust the leveling legs with a wrench.
- [Washer lid switch](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=washer+lid+switch&tag=applianceh06-20): commonly replaced on top-loaders when the lid is closed but the washer still won't agitate or spin. Search with your model number.
- [Washer drive belt](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=washing+machine+drive+belt&tag=applianceh06-20): GE Factory Service says a loose, worn, or broken belt stops the drum on belt-drive models. Search with your model number.
- [HE laundry detergent](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=HE+laundry+detergent&tag=applianceh06-20): the right detergent helps prevent the over-sudsing that Whirlpool says can slow or stop the spin (Step 5).

## When to stop and call a pro

- **It still won't spin** after you've balanced the load, leveled the washer, cleared the suds, and confirmed it drains. LG and Samsung both say to request service at that point.
- **An unbalanced code keeps coming back with an empty drum.** LG says if uE, UE, or Ub shows during an empty Rinse and Spin, the washer needs repair.
- **The GE motor reset doesn't hold.** GE says if the washer still won't agitate after a reset, or stops again after a couple of loads, schedule a repair.
- **The Options knob on a GE top-loader doesn't beep** when you turn it. GE says that knob isn't working and needs service.
- **The lid switch probe or magnet is broken**, or you suspect a worn belt, motor, or control board. GE Factory Service says a controls problem needs expert attention.
- **The drain hose is damaged.** Samsung says to request service.
- **You smell burning or the breaker trips.** Unplug it and call a technician.
- **It's still under warranty or a protection plan.** Check that before booking anyone else.

## What does it cost?

These are published averages, not quotes. Your price depends on your city, the part, and the diagnostic fee.

- **Whirlpool** lists average out-of-warranty washer repairs at **$215 to $385** for parts and labor. It says these are 2020 averages from its W Service network in six metro areas (Baltimore, Boston, Orlando, Birmingham, Seattle, and New York) and that actual costs vary ([Whirlpool](https://www.whirlpool.com/repair/service.html)).
- **Sears Home Services** says the average washer repair costs **$150 to $350** depending on the problem. It's a repair company, so treat that as a vendor estimate. Sears also says repair is often more cost-effective than replacement if the washer is under 10 years old ([Sears Home Services](https://www.searshomeservices.com/repair/washer-repair-service)).

Ask for a written estimate and whether the diagnostic fee counts toward the repair.
