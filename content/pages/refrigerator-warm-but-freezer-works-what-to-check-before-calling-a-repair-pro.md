Your freezer is still cold but the milk is warm. That combination is a useful clue. In most refrigerators the fresh-food side doesn't make its own cold air: Whirlpool says most units use a shared cooling system in which cold air is generated in the freezer and pushed into the fresh-food section through vents ([Whirlpool](https://producthelp.whirlpool.com/Refrigeration/Full-Size_Refrigerators/Bottom_Freezer_Refrigerator/Operation/Temperature_Concerns/Freezer/Too_Cold_in_Freezer_-_Refrigerator)). LG says the same thing: in most refrigerators, cold air is generated in the freezer and flows into the refrigerator ([LG](https://www.lg.com/in/support/product-support/troubleshoot/help-library/cs-CT52006627-20155295251040/)). So when only the fridge side is warm, the cold is usually being made but not delivered.

If **both** sections are warm, start with our guide to a [refrigerator that isn't cooling](/refrigerator-not-cooling-what-to-check-before-calling-a-repair-pro/) instead. This page covers the freezer-cold, fridge-warm case.

> **Safety first.** Unplug the refrigerator (or switch off its breaker) before you remove any panel, clean behind it, or defrost it. Samsung warns not to use a hair dryer or any other heat source to speed up defrosting, because it will damage the refrigerator. Don't open or tamper with the sealed refrigerant system. If you smell burning, see sparks, or the breaker trips, unplug the fridge and call a technician.

## Step 1: Protect your food

- **Measure, don't guess.** FDA says the refrigerator should be at 40°F or below and the freezer at 0°F, and because few refrigerator controls show the actual temperature, it recommends a freestanding appliance thermometer ([FDA](https://www.fda.gov/food/buy-store-serve-safe-food/refrigerator-thermometers-cold-facts-about-food-safety)). Whirlpool suggests placing it in the center of the compartment, away from walls, doors, and vents ([Whirlpool](https://producthelp.whirlpool.com/Refrigeration/Full-Size_Refrigerators/Product_Info/Product_Assistance/Tips_for_Properly_Setting_the_Controls)).
- **Know the cutoff.** FoodSafety.gov's chart lists what to keep or discard once food has been at 40°F or above for more than 2 hours. Meat, poultry, seafood, milk, yogurt, eggs, soft cheeses, and cooked leftovers are on the discard list; hard cheeses, butter, and uncut fresh fruit and vegetables are kept. Never taste food to judge whether it's safe. "When in doubt, throw it out" ([FoodSafety.gov](https://www.foodsafety.gov/food-safety-charts/food-safety-during-power-outage)).
- **Use the freezer that still works.** Move the most perishable items into the cold freezer or into a cooler with ice while you troubleshoot.

## Step 2: Rule out settings and timing

1. **Check both controls.** Whirlpool's blog says to check both the freezer and the refrigerator controls because they tend to have separate settings, and that controls sometimes get changed when an item or a hand brushes against them ([Whirlpool blog](https://www.whirlpool.com/blog/kitchen/why-is-my-refrigerator-not-cooling.html)). Whirlpool and LG both give 37°F as a typical fridge setting, with the freezer at 0°F.
2. **Make sure the fridge side isn't turned off.** Whirlpool says some refrigerators have to be turned back on manually after a power outage or surge, or get switched off during cleaning ([Whirlpool](https://producthelp.whirlpool.com/Refrigeration/Full-Size_Refrigerators/All_Refrigerator/Operation/Temperature_Concerns/Too_Warm_in_Refrigerator_Compartment)). On Samsung top-freezer models, Samsung says to check that no compartment is in Off mode ([Samsung](https://www.samsung.com/us/support/troubleshoot/TSG10003479/)).
3. **Give changes time.** Whirlpool says to wait at least 24 hours between adjustments. Samsung says it can take up to 28 hours to reach a new setting if the doors stay closed. LG says a newly installed or serviced refrigerator can take 2 to 3 days to stabilize ([LG](https://www.lg.com/us/support/help-library/if-your-lg-refrigerator-is-not-cold-enough--20155457094933)).
4. **Don't panic during a defrost cycle.** GE says the compressor and fans don't run during defrost, which can last 30 to 45 minutes and happen once or twice a day depending on the model ([GE Appliances](https://products.geappliances.com/appliance/gea-support-search-content?contentId=21631)). A short warm-up during that time is normal.

## Step 3: Clear the air path between the freezer and the fridge

This is the free check that matters most for this symptom.

- **Find the vents in both sections.** Whirlpool says cold air enters and exits the refrigerator compartment from the freezer through vents, which are in different places depending on the model. Locate them in both compartments and make sure nothing is blocking them ([Whirlpool, top-freezer models](https://producthelp.whirlpool.com/Refrigeration/Full-Size_Refrigerators/Top_Freezer_Refrigerator/Operation/Temperature_Concerns/Too_Warm_in_Refrigerator_Compartment_-_Top_Freezer_Refrigerator)).
- **Don't forget the return vents.** Whirlpool says that if the return air vents are blocked by bulky items, the cycle is broken, which could lead to a warm refrigerator compartment.
- **Reorganize the freezer too.** LG says to organize the freezer as well, because restricted airflow in the freezer can stop the refrigerator from cooling.
- **Leave space.** Samsung recommends keeping food 2 inches clear of the interior vents. LG suggests keeping the refrigerator no more than 70% full. FDA also says to avoid overpacking, because cold air must circulate around refrigerated food. This is easy to miss during holiday weeks when the fridge is crammed.
- **Check the airflow control (some models).** On Whirlpool top-freezer models, an airflow control on the back wall of the freezer regulates how much air flows between the freezer and refrigerator. Whirlpool says to set it to the Recommended setting, adjust it only temporarily, and return it to Recommended afterward. Check your owner's manual to see if your model has one.

## Step 4: Check the fridge door and seals

A warm fridge with a cold freezer can simply mean the fridge door isn't sealing.

- **Make sure the door closes fully.** LG says to check that shelves are installed correctly, food isn't sticking out, and bottles in the door bins stand upright.
- **Do the paper test.** Samsung says to close the door on a piece of paper (or a dollar bill) and pull it out. Slight resistance means the seal is working. If it slips out easily, clean the seal with dish soap and a damp cloth and test again. Samsung also says frost or condensation on the inside edges near the seal points to a spot where the door isn't sealing ([Samsung](https://www.samsung.com/us/support/troubleshoot/TSG10003479/)).
- **Were the doors removed recently?** Whirlpool says doors that weren't reinstalled correctly can cause poor sealing, frost or moisture, and temperature swings.
- **Check the freezer door too.** LG says a freezer door left open can form heavy frost that blocks airflow and stops the refrigerator from cooling.

## Step 5: Look for frost on the freezer's back wall

If the vents are clear and the doors seal, the next most common cause is a frosted-over evaporator, the cooling coil that is usually behind a panel at the back of the freezer.

- **Why it happens.** GE says frost-free refrigerators run a defrost heater in the evaporator section at the back of the freezer that melts frost off the coils, usually once or twice a day ([GE Appliances](https://products.geappliances.com/appliance/gea-support-search-content?contentId=19471)). Repair Clinic, a parts retailer, explains that if the defrost heater fails, frost keeps building until the coils are plugged and airflow is restricted, and it names this as the most likely cause of a cold freezer with a warm fridge on Frigidaire models ([Repair Clinic](https://www.repairclinic.com/RepairHelp/How-To-Fix-A-Refrigerator/58-2--/Frigidaire-Refrigerator-Freezer-Is-Cold-But-Refrigerator-Is-Warm)).
- **What you can see.** Heavy frost on the freezer's back panel, or frost on the vents, is a sign. LG's support site lists frost buildup and a blocked evaporator or fan among the causes of a fridge that won't cool and suggests defrosting and clearing ice buildup.
- **A manual defrost is a test, not a fix.** Samsung's steps: remove perishables and empty the ice bucket, lay towels under the fridge, unplug it, prop the doors open, swap towels until they stop getting wet or at least 24 hours pass, then turn it back on and wait about 2 hours before putting food back. Samsung says a manual defrost clears ice around the fan, but only service will correct the original cause, so the ice will come back ([Samsung](https://www.samsung.com/us/support/troubleshoot/TSG10007113/)). If the fridge cools normally after defrosting and then warms again over the following days, that points to the defrost system.

## Step 6: Listen for the evaporator fan

The evaporator fan pushes cold air over the coil and into both sections. Repair Clinic says that if the evaporator fan isn't working, the freezer or refrigerator won't cool adequately, and that a fan that is unusually noisy or won't turn freely is commonly replaced. Samsung says a noise caused by ice buildup around the fan needs service. You can usually hear the fan running behind the freezer's back panel when the compressor is running. If it is silent, grinding, or getting louder over time, note that for the technician. Whirlpool's blog lists the evaporator fan among internal parts whose repair "should be left to a professional."

## Typical causes behind a warm fridge with a cold freezer

Once settings, vents, and seals are ruled out, the usual suspects are inside the cabinet. Repair Clinic lists a frosted-over evaporator (from a failed defrost heater, defrost thermostat, or control), a failed evaporator fan motor, a door that isn't closing, a faulty temperature control, a torn or twisted door gasket, and an air damper that is stuck closed. The damper is a flap that lets cold air from the freezer into the fridge on many models. We can't tell from the outside which one yours is, which is what a diagnostic visit is for.

## Parts and tools that commonly help with this

These are links to Amazon search results, not specific products, so you pick the exact part. **Match any part to your fridge's model number** (on the label inside the fridge) and check it against your owner's manual. **Unplug the fridge or switch off its breaker before any repair.** A part is only worth buying after the free checks above point to it.

- [Refrigerator and freezer thermometer](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=refrigerator+freezer+thermometer&tag=applianceh06-20): FDA recommends one for each section, so you know the fridge is at or below 40°F and the freezer at 0°F.
- [Evaporator fan motor](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=refrigerator+evaporator+fan+motor&tag=applianceh06-20): commonly replaced when the fan behind the freezer panel is silent, noisy, or won't spin freely. Search with your model number.
- [Defrost heater](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=refrigerator+defrost+heater&tag=applianceh06-20) or [defrost thermostat](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=refrigerator+defrost+thermostat&tag=applianceh06-20): commonly replaced when the evaporator keeps frosting over. Testing these takes a multimeter. If you're not comfortable with that, call a pro.
- [Air damper control](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=refrigerator+air+damper+control&tag=applianceh06-20): commonly replaced on models with a damper when it's stuck closed. Not every fridge has one.
- [Refrigerator door gasket](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=refrigerator+door+gasket&tag=applianceh06-20): commonly replaced when the fridge door fails the paper test after cleaning. Gaskets are model-specific.

## When to stop and call a pro

- **The fridge side is still warm after 24 to 28 hours** with correct settings, clear vents, and a door that passes the paper test.
- **Frost keeps coming back** on the freezer's back wall after a manual defrost. Samsung says only service corrects the original cause.
- **The evaporator fan is silent, grinding, or getting louder.**
- **An error code shows**, or Samsung's display is still blinking 28 hours after you adjusted the temperature. Samsung says to request service in both cases.
- **The seals are warped or damaged.** Samsung says to request service.
- **You smell burning, see sparks, or the breaker trips.** Unplug the fridge first.
- **It's under warranty or a protection plan.** Check that before booking anyone else.

## What does it cost?

These are published averages, not quotes. Your price depends on your city, the part, and the diagnostic fee.

- **Whirlpool** lists average out-of-warranty refrigerator repairs at **$230 to $360** for parts and labor. It says these are 2020 averages from its W Service network in six metro areas (Baltimore, Boston, Orlando, Birmingham, Seattle, and New York), that they exclude sealed-system repairs, and that actual costs vary with location, diagnostic fees, and parts ([Whirlpool](https://www.whirlpool.com/repair/service.html)).
- **Sears Home Services** says the average refrigerator repair costs about **$220**, and that actual costs depend on what needs to be repaired. It's a repair company, so treat that as a vendor estimate ([Sears Home Services](https://www.searshomeservices.com/repair/refrigerator-repair-service)).

We don't have sourced prices for individual parts like a fan motor or defrost heater, so we're not giving any. Ask for a written estimate and whether the diagnostic fee counts toward the repair. For repair versus replace, Sears's rule of thumb is that repair usually makes sense if the fridge is under 10 years old and the repair costs less than half the price of a new one.

## How to find a repair service

Start with the brand. Its service line knows your model and warranty. These links were all checked on November 24, 2026:

- **Whirlpool:** [online scheduling](https://www.whirlpool.com/repair/service.html) or [W Service](https://service.whirlpool.com/), 1-844-667-7221.
- **GE Appliances:** [schedule through Bodewell](https://products.geappliances.com/appliance/gea-support-search-content?contentId=16875), or call 1-800-432-2737.
- **Samsung:** [repair request portal](https://www.samsung.com/us/support/service/), or call 1-800-SAMSUNG.
- **LG:** [repair request page](https://www.lg.com/us/support/repair-service/schedule-repair).
- **Any brand:** [Sears Home Services](https://www.searshomeservices.com/repair/refrigerator-repair-service) says it repairs refrigerators no matter where you bought them.

Have your model and serial number ready (the label is usually inside the fridge). Tell the scheduler that the freezer is cold but the fridge side is warm, the temperatures your thermometer showed, whether you saw frost on the freezer's back wall, whether the fan is running, and what you already tried. GE says to keep the fridge plugged in if service is needed so the symptoms don't disappear before the technician arrives. Hours and phone numbers change, so confirm on the linked page before you call.
