Water under or inside the fridge is easy to ignore, but a slow leak can damage your floor. The good news: a lot of fridge leaks come from simple things like a clogged drain, a loose water line, a filter that isn't seated right, or a fridge that isn't tilted the right way. This guide helps you find where the water starts, do the safe checks yourself, and know when to call a technician.

> **Safety first.** Water and electricity don't mix. GE says to unplug the refrigerator before you troubleshoot an ice maker leak, to lower the risk of electric shock ([GE Appliances](https://www.geappliances.com/ge/service-and-support/refrigerators-freezers.htm)). Whirlpool says to unplug the fridge and close the water shutoff valve before you inspect the water line. That valve may be under the sink, in the basement, or behind the fridge ([Whirlpool](https://www.whirlpool.com/blog/kitchen/refrigerator-leaking-water.html)). If a water line is cracked or split, LG says to turn off the home's water valve to stop the leak until a technician can fix it ([LG](https://www.lg.com/us/support/help-library/lg-refrigerator-troubleshooting-a-leaking-refrigerator--20152715569705)). Wipe up standing water so no one slips.

## Step 1: Make sure it's really a leak

Samsung says many puddles turn out to be condensation or a spill nobody noticed, and that the most common place to find water is at the bottom of the fridge, below the crisper drawers ([Samsung](https://www.samsung.com/us/support/troubleshoot/TSG10003766/)).

- **Wipe it up and watch.** Samsung's advice is to clear the water away and see if it comes back. Condensation can show up if the fridge was recently off or without power.
- **Look for a spill.** LG says an open container, hot food put straight into the fridge, or a door left open in summer can all leave water inside ([LG](https://www.lg.com/us/support/help-library/lg-refrigerator-troubleshooting-leaking-inside-my-lg-refrigerator--20153123905830)). Samsung UK adds that melted ice cubes that fell under the fridge are often mistaken for leaks ([Samsung UK](https://www.samsung.com/uk/support/home-appliances/what-should-i-do-if-i-suspect-my-samsung-fridge-freezer-is-leaking-water/)).
- **A few drips from the dispenser can be normal.** Samsung says it's normal for the dispenser to drip for a few seconds after you dispense water. Hold the glass there 3 to 5 seconds longer.

## Step 2: Find where the water starts

GE says the location of the leak tells you a lot about the cause ([GE Appliances Factory Service](https://repair.geappliances.com/resources/faq/article/why-is-my-refrigerator-leaking)):

- **Front of the fridge, under the doors:** probably not the door seal. GE says it's more likely a blocked drain line.
- **Back of the fridge:** if your fridge has a water or ice dispenser, GE says a damaged water valve or water line is the most likely cause.
- **Bottom center:** GE says a cracked or badly positioned drain pan, or high humidity, is the usual cause. Drain pans vary by model, so GE says this is often best left to a pro.
- **Inside the fridge:** an interior drain line (often behind the crisper drawers) can get blocked.

## Step 3: Check the defrost drain

Your fridge melts its frost on a schedule, and the water runs down a drain to a pan underneath. If the drain clogs or freezes, the water backs up.

- **Signs of a blocked drain:** Whirlpool says a clogged defrost drain can make water pool inside the fridge and then leak past the bottom door seal onto the floor. The drain hole is usually at the bottom back of the freezer. LG says ice or water in the bottom of the freezer, or water in the drawer under the vegetable drawer of a 4-door fridge, can mean the drain is blocked.
- **Top-freezer fridges:** LG says a blocked freezer drain can make water drip from the ceiling of the fridge section or pool on its floor. LG's fix is to move your food to a cooler or spare fridge, put towels around the base, unplug the fridge, and leave both doors open until all the frost melts. That's at least 24 hours, and up to three days in winter. After you plug it back in, LG says it can take 24 to 48 hours to get cold again ([LG](https://www.lg.com/us/support/help-library/how-to-troubleshoot-water-leaking-from-the-ceiling-or-pooling-on-the-floor-in-your-lg-top-freezer-refrigerator--20155272207393)).
- **An interior drain line:** GE says hot water or a small plumber's snake can clear it, but if it keeps happening, it needs a closer look. Whirlpool lists a turkey baster and pipe cleaners among the things to have on hand.
- **Keep it clear.** Whirlpool says to make sure food in the freezer isn't covering the defrost drain. If water keeps building up inside or under the fridge, Whirlpool recommends a professional inspection.

## Step 4: Check the water line (fridges with ice or water)

Unplug the fridge and close the water valve first.

- **Look for damage.** Whirlpool says to check the line for tears or breaks, which mean the supply line needs replacing. GE says the fix may be as simple as tightening connections, or it may need new parts if there are holes or cracks.
- **Look for an ice clog.** Whirlpool says an ice blockage in the tubing looks like a clear, see-through plug. You can try leaving the fridge unplugged for about two hours so it melts. If the clog isn't see-through, Whirlpool says to have a pro look at it.
- **Check the fitting (LG).** LG says to look where the water line goes into the fitting on the back. Only one black line on the tube should show. If you see two, gently press the collar and push the tube in until only one line is partly visible. If the lines on the back are cracked, split, or kinked, LG says it needs a repair visit.
- **Did the doors come off recently?** Samsung says that if the dispenser started dripping after the doors were removed (for a move or install), the water line at the door hinge may not be connected properly.

## Step 5: Check the water filter

- **Is it seated right?** Whirlpool says a badly installed filter can cause dispenser drips. LG says to turn the filter clockwise until it locks, and never to push or pull it without turning, or you can damage the mount.
- **Is it genuine?** Samsung and LG both say leaks can come from a filter that isn't their own brand. LG says to check the O-rings on the end of the filter for stretching or cracks. Samsung says to replace a damaged or non-genuine filter, and to request service if the filter housing is damaged or it still leaks with the filter out.
- **"Leak Detected" on a GE display:** on GE bottom-freezer models that use the RPWFE filter, GE says replacing the filter fixes most of these. Make sure the filter is a genuine RPWFE and installed correctly. If the message stays, GE says you need service ([GE Appliances](https://products.geappliances.com/appliance/gea-support-search-content?contentId=23448)).

## Step 6: Dispenser and ice maker drips

- **Purge the air.** After a new filter or a new install, air in the line can cause drips. LG says to dispense two to three gallons of water. Samsung says to fill a large pitcher until it stops, up to three more times if the stream sputters.
- **Ice maker turned off?** LG says if the ice maker is off, empty the ice bin or the ice will melt and leak out of the dispenser. Make sure the ice maker door is fully closed.
- **Lever stuck?** LG says sticky residue (like a spilled soda) can hold the dispenser lever in. Lock the controls, work the lever a few times, and clean it with warm water and mild soap.
- **Water pressure.** Samsung says pressure that is too high or too low can stop the valve from closing. Its test: the dispenser should fill 6 ounces (3/4 cup) in under 10 seconds. Samsung says home water pressure should be between 30 and 130 psi. Call a plumber if it's too high.

## Step 7: Check that it tilts back slightly

Samsung says a correctly leveled fridge is **not** level front to back. It should tilt back a little, which helps the doors close and the water drain. A quick check: look under the front. If the moving wheels still touch the floor, the front isn't high enough. Whirlpool says an unlevel fridge can let water collect at the front of the drain pan and leak from the bottom. Your owner's manual explains how to adjust the legs on your model.

## Parts and tools that commonly help with this

These are links to Amazon search results, not specific products, so you pick the exact part. **Match any part to your fridge's model number** (on the label inside the fridge) and check it against your owner's manual. **Unplug the fridge and close its water valve before any repair.** A part is only worth buying after the checks above point to it.

- [Turkey baster](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=turkey+baster&tag=applianceh06-20) and [pipe cleaners](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=pipe+cleaners&tag=applianceh06-20): both on Whirlpool's list of things to have for clearing a defrost drain (Step 3).
- [Small level](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=torpedo+level&tag=applianceh06-20): to check that the fridge tilts back slightly (Step 7).
- [Refrigerator water supply line](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=refrigerator+water+supply+line&tag=applianceh06-20): commonly replaced when the line behind the fridge is torn or cracked. Check the fitting size your fridge and valve use.
- [Refrigerator water filter](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=refrigerator+water+filter&tag=applianceh06-20): search with your filter's part number. Samsung, LG, and GE all say non-genuine filters can cause leaks or errors, so buy the genuine filter for your model.
- [Refrigerator water inlet valve](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=refrigerator+water+inlet+valve&tag=applianceh06-20): GE names a damaged water valve as a likely cause of leaks from the back. Search with your model number, and call a pro if you're not comfortable with water and electrical connections.

## When to stop and call a pro

- **Water keeps coming back** after you've cleared the drain, checked the line and filter, and fixed the tilt.
- **The water line or filter housing is cracked or damaged.** Turn off the water valve until it's fixed. LG and Samsung both say this needs service.
- **The leak is from the bottom center** (the drain pan). GE says this is often best left to the pros.
- **There's water or ice under the crisper drawers on a GE fridge with a dispenser.** GE says this can mean the water storage tank or tubing is leaking, and service is recommended ([GE Appliances](https://products.geappliances.com/appliance/gea-support-search-content?contentId=39598)).
- **The defrost drain keeps freezing.** That can point to a problem with the defrost system, which is a technician's job.
- **"Leak Detected" stays on** a GE display after a genuine filter is installed correctly.
- **It's still under warranty or a protection plan.** Check that before booking anyone else.

## What does it cost?

These are published averages, not quotes. Your price depends on your city, the part, and the diagnostic fee.

- **Whirlpool** lists average out-of-warranty refrigerator repairs at **$230 to $360** for parts and labor. It says these are 2020 averages from its W Service network in six metro areas (Baltimore, Boston, Orlando, Birmingham, Seattle, and New York), that they exclude sealed-system repairs, and that actual costs vary ([Whirlpool](https://www.whirlpool.com/repair/service.html)).
- **Sears Home Services** says the average refrigerator repair costs about **$220**, and that actual costs depend on what needs fixing. It's a repair company, so treat that as a vendor estimate ([Sears Home Services](https://www.searshomeservices.com/repair/refrigerator-repair-service)).

Water damage to your floor or cabinets isn't included in these figures. Ask for a written estimate and whether the diagnostic fee counts toward the repair.

## How to find a repair service

Start with the brand. Its service line knows your model and warranty. These links were all checked on November 20, 2026:

- **Whirlpool:** [online scheduling](https://www.whirlpool.com/repair/service.html).
- **GE Appliances:** [schedule through Bodewell](https://products.geappliances.com/appliance/gea-support-search-content?contentId=16875).
- **Samsung:** [repair request portal](https://www.samsung.com/us/support/service/), or call 1-800-SAMSUNG.
- **LG:** [repair request page](https://www.lg.com/us/support/repair-service/schedule-repair).
- **Any brand:** [Sears Home Services](https://www.searshomeservices.com/repair/refrigerator-repair-service) says it repairs refrigerators whatever the brand or place of purchase.

Have your model and serial number ready (the label is usually inside the fridge). Tell the scheduler where the water shows up, whether your fridge has an ice maker or dispenser, any message on the display, and what you already tried. Hours and phone numbers change, so confirm on the linked page before you call.
