A dishwasher that won't start is especially painful the week of a big meal. The good news is that the manufacturers say many "dead" dishwashers are actually fine. The usual causes are a door that isn't fully latched, a control lock or delay start that's switched on, or a power problem like a tripped breaker, a GFCI outlet, or a wall switch. This guide walks through those checks in order, and tells you when to stop and call a technician.

First, notice which kind of "won't start" you have, because the checks are different:

- **Nothing lights up at all.** Start with Step 2 (power).
- **The lights and beeps work, but the cycle won't begin.** Start with Step 1 (door, locks, and settings).

> **Safety first.** Turn off the dishwasher's circuit breaker, or unplug it if it has a cord, before you reach under the dishwasher, behind the kick plate, or into the wiring. Don't touch wiring or the terminal box with the power on. If the breaker or fuse keeps tripping, GE says to contact an electrician ([GE Appliances](https://products.geappliances.com/appliance/gea-support-search-content?contentId=18905)). If you smell burning or see smoke, cut the power at the breaker and call a qualified technician.

## Step 1: The lights work, but it won't start

GE says if the display lights up and the buttons make sounds, the power source isn't the problem ([GE Appliances](https://products.geappliances.com/appliance/gea-support-search-content?contentId=17397)). Check these first.

- **Close the door firmly.** Every manufacturer puts this near the top of the list. Whirlpool says the dishwasher won't start or resume a cycle if the door isn't fully latched, and that on most models you'll hear a click when it latches ([Whirlpool](https://producthelp.whirlpool.com/Dishwashers/Dishwasher/Operation/Not_Starting%2F%2FNo_Operation/Not_Starting_or_Operating_-_Dishwasher)). GE says a rack that isn't pushed all the way in, or a pan handle sticking out, can stop the door from closing, and so can trim, insulation, the cabinet, or the countertop ([GE Appliances](https://products.geappliances.com/appliance/gea-support-search-content?contentId=17396)). Whirlpool adds that a lower rack put in backward can keep the door from latching ([Whirlpool](https://producthelp.whirlpool.com/Dishwashers/Product_Info/Dishwasher_Product_Assistance/Dishwasher_Not_Starting_or_Not_Operating)).
- **Top-control models: close the door quickly.** Sears Home Services says that on dishwashers with buttons on the top edge of the door, close the door within 3 seconds of pressing Start ([Sears Home Services](https://www.searshomeservices.com/repair/dishwasher-repair-service)).
- **Turn off the control lock (child lock).** Whirlpool says that when the control lock is on, all buttons are disabled, and the light flashes three times if you press a button. It can look like the dishwasher has no power. On many Whirlpool models, you turn it off by holding the Control Lock key for 3 seconds ([Whirlpool](https://producthelp.whirlpool.com/Dishwashers/Product_Info/Dishwasher_Product_Assistance/Using_Control_Lock)). On LG, **CL** or a lock icon means Child Lock is on, and it stays on even after the dishwasher is turned off. Turn the dishwasher on, then hold the Child Lock button for 3 seconds ([LG](https://www.lg.com/us/support/help-library/lg-dishwasher-why-is-cl-showing-on-the-display-and-buttons-not-responding--20155118238912)). Samsung says its padlock button is usually Heated Dry, Sanitize, or Child Lock, held for 3 seconds ([Samsung](https://www.samsung.com/us/support/troubleshoot/TSG10013655/)). Button combinations vary by model, so check your manual.
- **Cancel delay start.** GE says a delayed cycle shows the hours left on the display, for example **2H** or **4H**, and won't begin until that time passes. Whirlpool says to press Cancel/Drain to cancel a delayed start.
- **Check for demo, sleep, or Sabbath mode.** GE says some models have a Demo Mode, and some have a Sleep or Sabbath Mode that turns off all lights and sounds even though there's power. Whirlpool says to wake a sleeping dishwasher by opening and closing the door or pressing Start. Your manual has the steps for your model.
- **Press Start, don't hold it.** Samsung says holding Start for more than 3 seconds cancels the cycle and drains the dishwasher. LG says the same: holding Start for 3 seconds cancels the cycle, and dashes on the display mean it's draining, so wait ([LG](https://www.lg.com/us/support/help-library/lg-dios-dishwasher-panel-buttons-unresponsive--20154629484942)).
- **Give it a minute.** Samsung says its dishwashers drain for about 35 seconds before filling, so it can sound like it's running with no water coming in yet. That's normal.
- **Dry your hands.** Samsung says touch controls may not respond to wet or soapy hands, or a dirty button. Wipe the panel with a soft damp cloth and try again ([Samsung](https://www.samsung.com/us/support/troubleshoot/TSG10007200/)).

## Step 2: Nothing lights up (check the power)

- **The breaker or fuse.** GE says to check your panel for a tripped breaker or a blown fuse, which can look black or burned out. Use the correct size, and call an electrician if it keeps tripping or blowing. GE says the circuit may not have the right amperage for the dishwasher (15 or 20 amps) ([GE Appliances](https://products.geappliances.com/appliance/gea-support-search-content?contentId=18905)). LG says running the dishwasher at the same time as other high-power appliances, like an induction range or microwave, can trip the breaker.
- **The plug and outlet.** GE says not every dishwasher has a cord. If yours does, the outlet is usually in the cabinet under the sink or the cabinet next door. Make sure it's plugged in, and if it's a GFCI outlet with a Reset button, press Reset ([GE Appliances](https://products.geappliances.com/appliance/gea-support-search-content?contentId=17396)).
- **The wall switch.** Samsung and GE both say many kitchens have a light-style switch for the dishwasher, on the wall near the counter or in the cabinet under the sink. Make sure it's on.
- **The Power button.** Samsung says its dishwashers need the Power button pressed before you can choose or start a cycle.
- **Just installed?** GE says that if a newly installed dishwasher has no power, have the installer check it, and that the warranty doesn't cover installation problems. Samsung says the same: if the problem started at installation, contact the installer. Whirlpool says a loose or miswired terminal block or loose wire nuts can cause unresponsive controls on a new install ([Whirlpool](https://producthelp.whirlpool.com/Dishwashers/Dishwasher/Operation/Not_Starting%2F%2FNo_Operation/Controls_Not_Responding_-_Dishwasher)). That's a job for the installer or an electrician, not a DIY fix.

## Step 3: Make sure water can get in

- **Open the water valve.** Whirlpool says the supply valve under the sink should be turned fully counterclockwise to open, and that a closed or restricted valve can keep the dishwasher from starting or make it show an error ([Whirlpool](https://producthelp.whirlpool.com/Dishwashers/Dishwasher/Operation/Not_Starting%2F%2FNo_Operation/Not_Starting_or_Operating_-_Dishwasher)).
- **Check the flood float.** The flood float is a small part in the bottom of the tub that tells the dishwasher when to stop filling. GE says a stuck float can stop the dishwasher from starting. On GE models starting with GDF, GDT, PDT, CDT, DDT, ADT, or ZDT, remove the filters, lift the float straight up and out, make sure it's straight and not damaged, clear any debris, and put it back. GE says other models need a technician for this ([GE Appliances](https://products.geappliances.com/appliance/gea-support-search-content?contentId=37380)). Sears also says to clear any debris under the float so it moves up and down freely.
- **Leak protection.** GE says some models with an LCD display have a leak protection feature that can stop the dishwasher from running.
- **Humming but not running (GE).** For GE models starting with GDF, GDT, PDT, CDT, DDT, ADT, or ZDT, GE says to pour one pint of hot tap water into the bottom of the dishwasher, press Start, and let it run for 5 minutes ([GE Appliances](https://products.geappliances.com/appliance/gea-support-search-content?contentId=17397)).

## Step 4: Look for a code, then reset

- **Read the display first.** Samsung says blinking lights or an error code may mean the dishwasher can't start because of a fault, and to look up that code before anything else.
- **Then reset.** Whirlpool says to press Cancel, then turn off the breaker or unplug the dishwasher for about 1 minute, and turn the power back on ([Whirlpool](https://producthelp.whirlpool.com/Dishwashers/Product_Info/Dishwasher_Product_Assistance/Dishwasher_Not_Starting_or_Not_Operating)). Samsung says 1 to 5 minutes. But Samsung also warns that a reset clears error codes from memory, and that the problem will likely come back if you don't fix the cause first ([Samsung](https://www.samsung.com/us/support/answer/ANS10004788/)). So reset after the checks above, not instead of them.

## Parts and tools that commonly help with this

These are links to Amazon search results, not specific products, so you pick the exact part. **Match any part to your dishwasher's model number** (usually on a label on the edge of the door or the tub) and check it against your user manual. **Turn off power at the breaker and close the water valve before any repair.** A part is only worth buying after the free checks above point to it.

- [Outlet tester with GFCI button](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=gfci+outlet+tester&tag=applianceh06-20): for checking that a plug-in dishwasher's outlet has power (Step 2).
- [Non-contact voltage tester](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=non+contact+voltage+tester&tag=applianceh06-20): for confirming the power is really off before you work near wiring.
- [Dishwasher door latch](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=dishwasher+door+latch&tag=applianceh06-20): commonly replaced when the door won't click shut or the dishwasher doesn't sense it's closed. Search with your model number.
- [Dishwasher float or float switch](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=dishwasher+float+switch&tag=applianceh06-20): GE says a damaged flood float can be replaced on some models (Step 3). Search with your model number.
- [Dishwasher control board](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=dishwasher+control+board&tag=applianceh06-20): Sears lists defective control boards among the causes. Search with your model number, and have a pro confirm the diagnosis first, because a board is an expensive guess.

## When to stop and call a pro

- **It still won't start** after you've checked the door, the locks and delay start, the power, and the water valve. GE, Samsung, and LG all say to request service at that point.
- **The breaker or fuse keeps tripping or blowing.** GE says to call an electrician.
- **The panel is on, but nothing lights up when you press buttons.** LG says the unit may be malfunctioning and need an inspection.
- **The door won't latch** even with the racks pushed in and nothing in the way. Sears lists faulty door latch switches among the causes, and the switch is inside the door.
- **The flood float needs checking on a model that isn't owner-serviceable.** GE says that's a technician job.
- **It's newly installed.** Call the installer first. GE says the warranty doesn't cover installation problems.
- **You smell burning or see smoke.** Cut the power at the breaker and call a technician.
- **It's still under warranty or a protection plan.** Check that before booking anyone else.

## What does it cost?

These are published averages, not quotes. Your price depends on your city, the part, and the diagnostic fee.

- **Whirlpool** lists average out-of-warranty dishwasher repairs at **$200 to $450** for parts and labor. It says these are 2020 averages from its W Service network in six metro areas (Baltimore, Boston, Orlando, Birmingham, Seattle, and New York), and that actual costs vary ([Whirlpool](https://www.whirlpool.com/repair/service.html)).
- **Sears Home Services** says the average dishwasher repair can be **$150 to $200**, that replacing a blown dishwasher fuse may cost as little as $150, and that replacing an electronic control board can cost more than $400. It's a repair company, so treat these as vendor estimates ([Sears Home Services](https://www.searshomeservices.com/repair/dishwasher-repair-service)).

Ask for a written estimate and whether the diagnostic fee counts toward the repair.

Hours and phone numbers change, so confirm on the linked page before you call.
