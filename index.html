Appliance Repair Help

When a washer, dryer, refrigerator, or dishwasher quits, the first questions are always the same: is this something I can safely check myself, what is it likely to cost, and who do I call?

These guides answer those three questions for the four appliances people most often need help with. Each one starts with the safety steps (unplug it, shut off the water), walks through the checks that cost nothing, explains when it is time to stop and call a pro, and links to manufacturer and national repair services. Every price or fact links to its source, and anything we could not verify is left out.

General information only. Appliance repair can involve electricity, gas, water, and refrigerant, so follow your owner’s manual and call a qualified technician if you are unsure. We are not a repair company and do not endorse any listed service.
